OFFICIAL GOAT POLICY
(LIVESTOCK SURCHARGE FINE PRINT)

NOT A PET CASH ONLY CLOWN DECIDES

BY BEING WITHIN THE SERVICE AREA (defined as the greater metro area, adjacent metro areas, your street, and behind you) YOU ACKNOWLEDGE THAT YOU HAVE READ, HONKED AT, AND FAILED TO UNDERSTAND THIS POLICY. Asking about the goat policy constitutes acceptance of the goat policy. Not asking about the goat policy also constitutes acceptance of the goat policy. The marquee told you to ask.

ARTICLE I — DEFINITIONS

"Goat" means a goat, a goat-shaped surcharge, any livestock already at your house when the technician arrives, any livestock that arrives with the technician, and any situation the clown points at and says is a goat. The word "goat" is not a term of endearment. It is a line item.

"Pet" means something you were allowed to keep. The goat is not a pet. If you have begun feeding it, that is a separate invoice.

"You" means the person who did not request this, the person who did, the homeowner, the landlord, the studio audience, and anyone standing near a balloon.

"Free balloon" means the balloon that is free if we do not arrive in 30 minutes. It has never been free. It does not cover the goat. It will never cover the goat.

"The clown" means the technician on duty. The clown decides.

ARTICLE II — THE GOAT IS A SURCHARGE

The goat is a livestock surcharge. It is not a companion animal, a party favor, a gift, a mascot, or "part of the act." The rate card says see goat because the goat cannot be priced in advance. The goat is priced when the clown sees the goat, or when the goat sees you, whichever happens first.

ARTICLE III — SOMETIMES THE GOAT IS ALREADY AT YOUR HOUSE

  1. If a goat is at your house when the technician arrives, that goat is now the surcharge. We do not need to have brought it. Presence is delivery.
  2. If no goat is at your house, one may be provided. Provision is not a request you get to make. Provision is a finding of fact by the clown.
  3. If you already had a goat and you thought it was yours, see Article I. It is our goat now for billing purposes. Ownership for all other purposes is the goat's business.
  4. Neighbors' goats count. Goats visible from the driveway count. A painting of a goat counts at half rate. A balloon goat is a balloon, not a goat, and is billed under balloons.
  5. Denial that there is a goat is a goat.

ARTICLE IV — THE FREE BALLOON DOES NOT COVER THE GOAT

Our guarantee ("WE ARRIVE IN 30 MINUTES OR THE BALLOON IS FREE") applies only to one (1) balloon animal of simple construction (dog or snake). The guarantee does not apply to:

  • goats;
  • goat-adjacent livestock;
  • any situation in which a goat is already at your house, which is most situations;
  • balloons eaten, sat on, or juggled by a goat;
  • the silent approach;
  • leaving when asked;
  • Glendale tapings, studio audiences, or corporate clients;
  • anything you bought, or tried to buy, on Screan.

If the balloon is free, the goat is still due. If the goat eats the free balloon, you owe for the balloon and the goat. This is the policy.

ARTICLE V — CASH ONLY

We accept cash. We do not accept checks, cards, goats as payment for goats, IOUs, balloon animals, Screan gift cards, residual checks from The Tyler Lofftus Show, or good intentions. We do not take checks drawn on a goat. HONK-4-U is a dispatch line, not a billing department. Do not read a card number into the honk.

All surcharges are due when the clown decides the job is done. The job is not done if the goat is still there, unless the clown says the job is done, in which case the goat remaining is a new job.

ARTICLE VI — THE CLOWN DECIDES

The clown decides:

  1. whether there is a goat;
  2. what the goat costs;
  3. whether you already had it;
  4. when the job is done;
  5. whether you may take a photograph (no);
  6. whether this paragraph applies to you (yes).

Appeals may be filed by honking. Appeals are denied by honking. There is no third honk.

ARTICLE VII — WAIVERS, INDEMNITY, AND YOUR CARPET

You waive all claims arising from hoof marks, greasepaint on livestock, livestock on furniture, unexpected bleating during a taping, or the discovery that the goat has been in the guest room since Tuesday. SANBAR EMERGENCY CLOWN DISPATCH is not liable for goats that were already at your house, goats that followed the technician home from a previous callout, or goats that appear in the background of family photographs taken years before dispatch. If a goat appears in an old photograph, that is a pre-existing goat and is billable as a finding.

You agree the clown was invited. You agree the goat was invited. You agree that "I did not request this" is a testimonial, not a refund request.
